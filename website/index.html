01

Geometry

The bottom of the desktop stays readable. The top recedes — about 11.5% inset, progressive blur, critically damped follow.

Lid 72°

Drag the control, or move over the frame.

02

Tracking

The first source that reports a live 0–180° angle wins. A hardware hinge or IMU always beats the webcam. The camera never opens on a desktop tower.

IIO hinge
ChromeOS lid-angle, Intel HID hinge, and other in_angl* channels.
Dual accelerometer
Convertibles: the hinge is the angle between lid and base gravity vectors.
Display IMU
Closed is 0°. A typical pose is about 100°. Flat is 180°. Gravity from the panel IMU.
HID lid angle
Apple HID and other sensor-page lid devices. A udev rule ships in the zip.
Lid webcam
No IMU. Vertical shift from the camera in the lid becomes pitch. LED on only while Hinge is on.
Lid switch
Binary open or closed. Closing can still play the fold, then sleep.
03

Install

  1. Get the tree

    Download the zip, or clone the repository.

  2. Install the extension

    cd hinge
make install
gnome-extensions enable hinge@agayushh.github.io

  3. Log out on Wayland

    A first-time install may need a new session. Then open Hinge in the top bar and turn it on.

  4. Set open position

    Sit comfortably and press Set open position. Toggle with Ctrl Alt H.

If a HID lid sensor exists but is not readable, copy 99-hinge-lid.rules into /etc/udev/rules.d/ and replug. The panel and Ubuntu Dock stay put. Fullscreen games that unredirect are brought back under the compositor while the fold is visible.
